Official Directives

General Rules & Regulations

These regulations apply across every competitive category at the symposium. Please review them thoroughly before registering.

01

Eligibility & ID

  1. All participants must carry a valid physical institutional ID card at all times throughout the fest.
  2. Participation is open to bona fide undergraduate and postgraduate students from recognized colleges and universities.
  3. In group events, all team members must represent the same institution. Inter-college composite teams are not permitted.
  4. A maximum of three teams from the same institution may register for any single competitive event.
02

Campus Conduct

  1. Strict code of conduct applies on campus grounds. Participants must follow instructions from volunteers and faculty marshals.
  2. Zero tolerance towards alcohol, narcotics, smoking, e-cigarettes, or any banned substances. Violators face immediate disqualification and removal.
  3. Any intentional damage to college infrastructure, lab equipment, or stage AV systems will invite disciplinary action and full financial recovery.
  4. Participants are solely responsible for their personal belongings, kits, and laptops. Organizers bear no liability for loss or theft.
03

Competitions & Timing

  1. Participants must report to their assigned venue desk at least 30 minutes before the scheduled event time. Late check-ins may be forfeited.
  2. Vulgarity, obscenity, defamation, or inappropriate content in any form (music, speeches, outfits, or visuals) is strictly prohibited.
  3. Performers and teams must clear their equipment, props, and stage gear immediately upon round conclusion.
  4. The decision of the designated judges and evaluation panel is final, absolute, and binding on all contenders.
04

Awards & Certification

  1. All registered contenders will receive an official, authorized Certificate of Participation issued by Shree Daksha Academy.
  2. Winners and runners-up in each of the 12+ competitions are awarded trophies, merit certificates, and cash prizes from the ₹1 Lakh pool.
  3. Prizes and honors will be conferred during the valedictory ceremony on campus at the conclusion of the events.
  4. All digital certificates can be accessed directly from the official portal post-event.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about eligibility, payments, certificates, and event day logistics.

Who is eligible to participate in the symposium events?

Any bona fide student currently enrolled in an accredited undergraduate or postgraduate institution with a valid institutional identity card is eligible to compete.

Can students from different colleges form a combined team?

For all group events, all team members must represent the same institution. Inter-college composite teams are not permitted unless explicitly authorized in the individual event guidelines.

How do I pay the registration fee and confirm my entry?

You can submit an online UPI reference and payment screenshot, or choose cash at the campus registration desk. Online submissions stay pending until organizers verify payment. Demo mode uses test details only; do not send real money.

Can I register for multiple competitions?

Yes! Participants are welcome to register for multiple competitions, provided the scheduled time slots and venues do not directly conflict. Please verify the event timings before confirming your registration.

What prizes and awards will participants receive?

Winners across all 12+ competitions will receive cash awards from the cumulative ₹1 Lakh prize pool, trophies, and merit certificates.

Will participants receive a certificate?

Yes. Every eligible attendee receives an official, high-resolution digital Certificate of Participation powered by our Daksha Infinix platform. Winners also receive physical trophies, cash prizes, and merit certificates.

Is spot registration available on the day of the event?

Yes, spot registration opens at 8:30 AM at the central campus registration desk on event days. However, slots are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis, so online pre-registration is strongly encouraged to ensure slot availability.

Is there an entry fee to attend as a spectator or supporter?

Campus entry is completely free for all students carrying a valid college ID card who wish to cheer for their institutional delegations. Entry fees apply solely to registered participants in competitive events.

Whom do I contact for event-specific queries or technical support?

For event-specific questions, open the "Details" button on an event card or message the official symposium desk on WhatsApp. Individual coordinator numbers will appear here once confirmed.
